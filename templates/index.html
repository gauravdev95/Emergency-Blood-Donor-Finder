How it works

1

Request

Enter the blood group needed and the patient's locality.

2

Match

HashMap finds compatible donors instantly, unavailable donors are filtered out.

3

Rank

Dijkstra road distance + Min-Heap puts the nearest donor first.

4

Contact

Reach donors who consented to share their contact details.

Example: a patient in Agra needing O+ gets compatible donors (O+ and O−) ranked by distance — e.g. Sikandra, Kamla Nagar, Dayal Bagh — with incompatible groups filtered out.

⚡

Fast in emergencies

Real road routes

Dijkstra on the Agra locality graph, not just straight-line distance.

Privacy-first
